eBookSync

A comic reader for the TI-84 Plus CE.

Comics are converted on a computer into
a format a 48MHz eZ80 can draw, and sent
over a USB serial link.

KEYS

  Book list
    enter   open a book
    del     mark a book read
    2nd     sync with a computer
    mode    settings
    clear   quit

  Reading
    arrows  pan, hold to speed up
    + -     zoom
    mode    fit width / full zoom
    del     mark read
    clear   back

  Anywhere
    2nd+on  lock the calculator

LOCKING

2nd+on turns the screen off. Press on to
wake it: your wallpaper, the date and
time, and the password prompt.

Three wrong answers turn it off again
and it comes back still locked. There is
no way past it from the keypad, so this
locks you out too. The ways out are the
password, the batteries, or the sync
page on a computer.

With no password set it is just a screen
blanker and any key returns.

Settings has a "Lock at power-on"
switch.
With it on, turning the calculator on
asks for the password first, wherever
you were. It needs a password and a sync
before it will turn on.

If it ever goes wrong, clear the RAM:
hold del while putting a battery back.
