eOS

A comic reader for the TI-84 Plus CE.

Comics are converted on a computer into
a format a 48MHz eZ80 can draw, and sent
over a USB serial link.

KEYS

  Book list
    enter   open a book
    del     mark a book read
    2nd     sync with a computer
    mode    settings
    clear   quit

  Reading
    arrows  pan, hold to speed up
    + -     zoom
    mode    fit width / full zoom
    del     mark read
    clear   back
