How to connect
  1. Run EOS on the calculator.
  2. Press 2nd on the book list to open the Sync screen.
  3. Plug the calculator in and press Connect calculator.
  4. Pick the calculator's serial port from the browser's list.

On OS 5.5 and later the calculator needs the arTIfiCE jailbreak before it will run native programs at all.
